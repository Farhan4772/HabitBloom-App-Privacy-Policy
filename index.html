HabitBloom — Privacy Policy

Habit tracking, without the tracking.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

HabitBloom is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. In short: HabitBloom does not collect, store, or share any personal data on a remote server. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

No ads No account required No data sold or shared Works offline

Information We Collect

HabitBloom does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal information to use the app. The only information the app works with is:

  • The habit names and icons you choose to create
  • The dates you mark a habit as completed

This information is created by you, for you, and is stored entirely on your own device using a local, on-device database. It is never transmitted to HabitBloom, its developer, or any third party.

How Information Is Used

The habit and completion data stored on your device is used only to:

  • Show your habit list and daily progress
  • Calculate your streaks
  • Display your garden and calendar history

This data is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics of any kind.

Data Sharing

HabitBloom does not share, sell, rent, or transmit your data to any third party, advertiser, or analytics service, because your data never leaves your device in the first place.

Advertising

HabitBloom does not display advertisements and does not integrate with any advertising network.

Permissions

HabitBloom does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device permission beyond what is required for the app to run.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual habits at any time from within the app, which permanently removes that habit and its history from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining data stored by HabitBloom, since no copy exists anywhere else.

Children's Privacy

HabitBloom does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated in the future, the new version will be published at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date above.
